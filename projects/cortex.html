Featured Project

Cortex

Cortex is an AI-powered language learning coach that delivers personalized practice across reading, listening, writing, speaking, and vocabulary, with adaptive feedback to help learners improve faster for real-world communication and exam success.

The Problem With Old Language Apps

  • Most legacy apps are one-size-fits-all and not truly customizable.
  • They are often not interactive enough and can feel repetitive or boring.
  • They are disconnected from where learners now spend time: inside AI assistants.

Learning In The Age Of AI

  • AI enables anyone to learn almost anything on demand.
  • But without structure, AI responses do not guarantee consistent progression.

What AI Needs: Structure

  • A knowledge base that dictates course quality and direction.
  • Backend systems for learner memory, progression, and continuity.
  • Gamified feedback loops that make learning engaging and sticky.

What Cortex Delivers

  • Deep personalization based on learner profile, goals, and history.
  • Real-time AI tutoring tailored to when, where, and how the learner studies.
  • Interactive practice across reading, listening, writing, speaking, and vocabulary.
  • Structured API/MCP architecture for extensible learning workflows.

Demo Focus

  • Show onboarding and personalization setup.
  • Generate and complete an interactive learning session.
  • Capture feedback, memory, and progression updates.

Future Vision

  • Any learning journey can become AI-native.
  • Cortex is the structure layer that makes AI learning reliable and scalable.
